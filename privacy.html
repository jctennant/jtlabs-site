Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy describes how JT Labs LLC ("JT Labs", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our apps and this website.

Information we collect

Unless an individual app's listing states otherwise, our apps do not collect personal information. Data you create in our apps stays on your device or in your own iCloud account.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
